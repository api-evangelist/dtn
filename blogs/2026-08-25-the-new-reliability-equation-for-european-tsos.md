Electrification, renewable generation, and cross-border interdependence are changing the operating environment for European TSOs and the assumptions behind reliable grid operations. For decades, European transmission system operators (TSOs) operated with relatively predictable generation sources, established demand patterns, and conservative operating assumptions. Those conditions are changing.
