DTN is an operational intelligence company delivering weather, agriculture, energy, and commodity market data through a catalog of 36 published APIs on its developer portal. Coverage spans current conditions, point and probabilistic forecasts, 30-year station observation history, lightning, radar and map tiles, tropical cyclones, marine weather, aviation weather (METARs, TAFs, NOTAMs, PIREPs, SIGMETs, flight hazards), soil conditions, renewables production forecasting, refined fuels, and Prophet X commodity market data. All APIs authenticate with OAuth2 client credentials via the DTN Auth and Identity Service, publish OpenAPI specs and Postman collections, and stream real-time data over WebSocket and HTTP streaming surfaces.

**Areas:** Weather · Agriculture · Energy · Market Data · Aviation · Marine · Forecasting · Observations · Commodities · Real-Time

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for DTN: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
